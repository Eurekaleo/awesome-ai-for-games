06 · Test & Evaluate

AI and Foundation Models for Automated Game Testing and Evaluation

How intelligent agents explore games, verify mechanics, discover defects, judge quality, and produce evidence grounded in player behavior.

Direct answer

AI and foundation models for automated game testing use intelligent agents and learned evaluators to explore game behavior, exercise mechanics, identify failures, and assess player-facing quality. Automated playtesting agents may navigate from pixels, GUI elements, structured state, or engine APIs while pursuing coverage, novelty, progress, or targeted test objectives. Exploration and judgment are separate problems: reaching an unusual state does not show that the system can recognize a defect, and a strong oracle cannot help if relevant states are never visited. Verification methods therefore combine action generation with executable assertions, state invariants, regression selection, crash signals, visual checks, or model-based judges. Foundation models can interpret requirements, generate tests, summarize traces, or judge open-ended behavior, but their verdicts require calibration, agreement analysis, and meta-evaluation against trusted evidence. Testing also includes human relevance. Procedural personas or diverse agents may broaden behavior, yet diversity alone does not establish that they represent real players. Evaluation should report validated defects, behavioral or state coverage, verdict accuracy, reproduction cost, false positives, held-out defect families, and performance under realistic time and interaction budgets.

Evidence

What should evaluation establish?

Testing evidence should connect exploration to independently validated outcomes. Strong studies use held-out defects, calibrated oracles, relevant player samples, and reproducible traces while reporting the cost and false positives of each discovered issue.

  • Validated defects and state coverage
  • Oracle or verdict accuracy
  • Reproduction time and test cost
  • Held-out defects and player relevance
